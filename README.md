Software developer in Curitiba, Brazil. I work as an ML/AI developer at [Kinebot](https://kinebot.com.br) (computer vision and LLM applications), and in my free time I build tools I use every day: apps for my Mac and iPhone, a practice app for guitar, my band's setlist, and the cards I use to learn German.

- [sudoku-heuristica](https://github.com/viniciusbregoli/sudoku-heuristica): a Sudoku solver with greedy and A* search, in Pygame.
- [anki-highlighting-addon](https://github.com/viniciusbregoli/anki-highlighting-addon) and [youtube-speed-controller-extension](https://github.com/viniciusbregoli/youtube-speed-controller-extension): small tools for Anki and YouTube.
